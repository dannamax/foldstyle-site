Privacy Policy

中文

Last updated: September 14, 2026

FoldStyle creates lock screen wallpapers on your device. We built it so that everything happens locally, which means there is very little data to talk about.

Data we collect

None. FoldStyle has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporting service. We do not receive your name, email address, contacts, location or any identifier about your device.

Photos

When you pick a photo as a wallpaper background, the image stays on your device. It is used only to render the wallpaper you asked for and is never uploaded. Wallpapers you save are written to your own Photos library with your permission, and only you can delete them.

On-device storage

  • Your template, accent theme and purchase status are stored locally, and shared with the FoldStyle widget through the iOS App Group so the widget can match your choice.
  • The widget reads the battery level that iOS itself provides. That number never leaves your device.

Purchases

FoldStyle Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Restoring a purchase is handled by Apple as well.

Third parties

FoldStyle contains no third-party tracking, analytics or advertising components.

Children

FoldStyle is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app and the date above is updated.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@foldstyle.app
